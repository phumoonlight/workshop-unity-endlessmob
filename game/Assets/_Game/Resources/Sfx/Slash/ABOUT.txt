Sword swing sounds, 28 takes, from a free sound pack downloaded by the
developer (originally imported as Assets/SwordSoundPack).

Write the pack's name, author and licence here before the game is shared --
the folder it came in was the only record of where these are from, and moving
the files here lost it.

GameAudio picks one of these at random for every sword swing, never the same
one twice in a row. Every clip here is a *swing*: silent for its first tenth
of a second, then swelling to a peak around 0.3s. That is why MeleeSlash
starts the sound at the wind-up instead of at the moment of impact.

Adding or deleting takes needs no code -- the folder is read at runtime.
